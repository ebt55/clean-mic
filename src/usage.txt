clean-mic: echo-cancelling microphone for the QuadCast (docs/design.md)

  clean-mic devices
      List playback and capture endpoints (opens no stream).

  clean-mic live [--mic QuadCast] [--speakers NAME] [--seconds 180] [--out DIR]
                 [--no-aec] [--no-rnnoise] [--wait-ms 20] [--bulk-delay-ms 0]
      Record the mic and the default playback's loopback, cancel the echo,
      write a run folder (default %LOCALAPPDATA%\clean-mic\runs\run-<time>)
      and print ERLE, lag, drift, CPU and latency every 2 s.

  clean-mic replay RUN_DIR [--out DIR] [--no-aec] [--no-rnnoise] [--wait-ms 20]
                 [--bulk-delay-ms 0] [--quiet]
      Re-run a recorded run folder through the chain.

  clean-mic bench [--seconds 60]
      CPU cost of the chain on a synthetic scene.

  clean-mic wer [--passage docs/passages.md] [--span-from RAW.words.json] A.words.json ...
      Word error rate of transcripts against the reading passage.

  --bulk-delay-ms: positive delays the reference (Bluetooth or HDMI output),
  negative delays the mic (a lookahead; adds that much latency).
